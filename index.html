Welcome to Inkvitation Tattoo

Inkvitation Tattoo is an intimate tattoo studio located in the charming town of Ballinasloe. It has been open and operated since 2023 by the skilled artist Łukasz Konsek, also known as Kons.


Kons is a passionate tattoo artist who began his career in 2009. With valuable experience gained from working in various studios, he eventually fulfilled his dream of owning his own Inkvitation Tattoo studio.


Despite his modesty, Kons has garnered numerous awards over the years, showcasing his undeniable artistic skills and knowledge. This has made him a sought-after choice among clients from the entire region. If you are looking for a place where professionalism and passion blend with subtlety and aesthetics, you've come to the right place.

Hygiene and Safety

At INKVITATION TATTOO, your safety and well-being are our top priorities. We strictly adhere to the highest standards of hygiene and infection control, following all recommended guidelines for tattoo studios.

Every needle and tube is single-use and disposed of safely after each session. All surfaces and equipment are thoroughly disinfected before and after every client. We use only premium, sterile inks and supplies to ensure both safety and vibrant results.

Our studio is regularly inspected and maintained to exceed industry standards. We provide professional aftercare instructions and offer trusted tattoo care products to support proper healing.

Whether you’re getting a new tattoo or a cover-up, you can relax knowing your experience will be safe, clean, and comfortable. We are committed to creating custom artwork in a secure environment, so you can enjoy your tattoo with confidence.
