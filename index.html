About Us

Inkvitation Tattoo is an intimate tattoo studio located in the charming town of Ballinasloe. It has been open and operated since 2023 by the skilled artist Łukasz Konsek, also known as Kons.


Kons is a passionate tattoo artist who began his career in 2009. With valuable experience gained from working in various studios, he eventually fulfilled his dream of owning his own Inkvitation Tattoo studio.


Despite his modesty, Kons has garnered numerous awards over the years, showcasing his undeniable artistic skills and knowledge. This has made him a sought-after choice among clients from the entire region. If you are looking for a place where professionalism and passion blend with subtlety and aesthetics, you've come to the right place.
